Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 12, 2026

BudgetESF ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and handled when you use the BudgetESF mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Analytics & Performance Data

We collect limited, non-personal analytics data to understand how the App is used and to improve performance, stability, and user experience. This data may include:

  • App usage events (for example, feature usage or interaction events)
  • App performance metrics (such as crashes or errors)
  • Device and operating system information (general, non-identifying)

This data is collected using third-party analytics services (such as Firebase Analytics) and is used strictly for performance measurement and product improvement. We do not use analytics data for advertising, tracking across apps, or selling user data.

1.2 User-Provided Information

If you choose to contact us through the App (for example, via feedback or support forms), you may voluntarily provide:

  • Your email address
  • Any information you include in your message
This information is collected solely so that we can respond directly to your inquiry.

2. How We Use Information

  • To improve app performance and stability
  • To understand feature usage
  • To respond to user support or feedback inquiries

3. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect. Analytics data is handled by trusted third-party services that follow industry-standard security practices. Email addresses and feedback messages are used only for correspondence and are not retained longer than necessary.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except:

  • Analytics providers used for app performance and stability
  • When required by law or legal process

No personally identifiable information is shared for commercial purposes.

5. Your Choices

You can use the App without providing any personal information.

  • Providing an email address for feedback or support is optional.
  • You may stop using the App at any time.

Changes to This Policy
